01 / Profile

Engineer the system.
Interrogate the evidence.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from United International University, Bangladesh. I work where applied AI, software architecture, experimental discipline, and human-centered product design meet.

From difficult idea to inspectable product.

My work spans deep learning, NLP, multimodal systems, neuro-symbolic reasoning, full-stack development, research tooling, computational genomics, Green AI, and interactive visualization. I enjoy building the complete path: data and evidence, model or domain logic, APIs, persistence, interface, evaluation, documentation, and failure handling.

I care about what happens after a model produces a number. Where did the evidence come from? What assumptions shaped it? What happens when a modality is missing? Can a user understand the result? Can someone reproduce the experiment? Those questions guide how I engineer systems.

A strong intelligent system should not only work—it should make its data flow, uncertainty, trade-offs, and user impact understandable.

Flagship · Final Year Design Project

Synapse Atlas

Neuro-Symbolic Multimodal AI for Interpretable Depression Screening

A local-first research platform built by Team Reboot to make mental-health screening predictions inspectable. The retained pathway predicts eight ordinal PHQ-8 symptom concepts from text, then computes the total and research screening output through explicit symbolic rules. Audio, video, proxy-anxiety, and distress evidence remain separately attributed instead of being silently fused into one opaque score.

  • MentalRoBERTa concept pathway with explicit ordinal symptom concepts and deterministic PHQ rules.
  • Whisper ASR and WavLM supporting evidence for audio/video workflows, with modality boundaries kept visible.
  • Concept interventions, uncertainty display, provenance-aware evidence exploration, and local-first operation.
  • Synapse Guide: result-aware local Qwen3 4B assistant using llama.cpp and curated retrieval.

04 / Technology stack

Breadth for building.
Depth where it matters.

I choose tools around the problem rather than forcing every project into one stack. The repeated pattern is end-to-end ownership, explicit architecture, and measurable validation.

How I work

My engineering process combines product thinking with research discipline.

  1. Frame the problem before choosing the model or framework.
  2. Make assumptions, provenance, and evaluation boundaries explicit.
  3. Separate research evidence from demo behavior and product polish.
  4. Build the full path from interface to persistence to intelligence.
  5. Test failure paths and document what the system cannot claim.
